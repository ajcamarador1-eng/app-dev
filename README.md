# Interstellar - My Favorite Movie

**Interstellar** is a sci-fi movie by Christopher Nolan about astronauts traveling through space to find a new home for humanity.

## Why I Love It

- Amazing visual effects
- Great story about love and survival
- Cool science about black holes and time
- Emotional father-daughter relationship

## Main Characters

- Cooper (Matthew McConaughey)
- Dr. Brand (Anne Hathaway)
- Murph (Jessica Chastain)

## Favorite Quote

> "We used to look up at the stars, now we just worry about the dirt."
